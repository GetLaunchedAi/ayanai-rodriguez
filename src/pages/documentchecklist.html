What You Need To Get Started

Here is a checklist of all the official documents that you will need to gather in order to complete your tax return, as well as of the specific forms that apply to each individual situation. Use it as a personal tool to help you prepare for the tax filing process.

Required Documents For Everyone

  • Government-issued photo ID
    Driver’s license, state ID, or passport
  • Prior federal & state returns
    Last year’s (helpful: prior 2 years)
  • Social Security card
    For you, spouse, and dependents
  • IRS Letters/Notices
    1444-C, 6475 (stimulus) if applicable

Have a W-2 / am employed:

  • W-2 wage statements
    One from each employer

Self-employed

  • 1099-NEC / 1099-MISC
    Business/farm income & deductions
  • Own a small business
    1099s, expense records, K-1 (if any)

Have children and/or dependents

  • SSNs for each child
    Plus Letter 6419 (Advance CTC) if received

Own a home

  • Mortgage & property tax
    Form 1098, tax bill, or mortgage stmt

Investment income

  • Interest / dividends
    1099-INT, 1099-OID, or 1099-DIV
  • Tuition
    Form 1098-T

Additional Documents Based on Your Individual Situation

  • Sold a house
    Closing docs or Form 1099-S
  • Own a rental property
    Income & expenses (e.g., 1099-MISC)
  • Have ACA health insurance
    Form 1095-A
  • Sold stocks or mutual funds
    Form 1099-B; include cost basis
  • Have student loans
    Form 1098-E (interest)
  • Donated to a charity
    Receipts/acknowledgments for gifts
  • Federally declared disaster
    Loss details, repair/replace costs
